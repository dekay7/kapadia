Link Inspector

Inspect URL safety and trace redirect chains before you click. Fetching runs at the edge — your browser never contacts the target.

Privacy: URLs are fetched by the Cloudflare edge — your browser never contacts the target. Third-party services used server-side: Cloudflare DNS-over-HTTPS (cloudflare-dns.com) for SSRF pre-resolution, and RDAP (rdap.org) for domain registration data. No URLs or results are logged or stored by kapadia.org.
